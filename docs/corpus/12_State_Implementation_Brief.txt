State Implementation Brief: Connected Services
==============================================

Purpose and reporting period
----------------------------
This brief describes a separate connected-services pilot in the Aster and
Bhagirathi districts during financial year 2024-25. It links assisted digital
service points with housing referrals, payment correction and clean-energy
applications. It should not be merged with the Integrated District Delivery
Pilot in the other casebook.

Financial position
------------------
The state approved an allocation of Rs 42 crore for the pilot. By the end of
2024-25, Rs 25 crore had been released and Rs 23.9 crore had been spent. The
release number covers funds transferred to implementing agencies; expenditure
includes operations and training. The brief gives no monthly expenditure series
and no 2025-26 expenditure figure.

People and service records
--------------------------
Service points recorded 22,000 enrolled households. Identity and eligibility
checks were complete for 17,500 households. A payment or service transaction was
successfully completed for 14,200 households at least once. Enrolment, verified
eligibility and successful transaction are separate measures. A household may be
enrolled without being verified, or verified without having a successful
transaction during the reporting window.

Clean-energy referrals
----------------------
The pilot received 3,600 clean-energy referrals. Of these, 2,400 passed the
initial eligibility review and 1,760 installations were verified by inspection.
Some eligible referrals remained pending because of wiring work, vendor capacity
or an incomplete bank detail. The word "approved" in the local tracker refers to
the initial eligibility review, not to a completed installation.

Housing and payment support
---------------------------
Housing referrals were sent to urban local bodies for local verification. The
brief reports 1,200 referrals, but does not report how many houses were finally
completed. Payment corrections were opened for 860 records; 610 were closed by
the reporting date and the remainder were still in the correction queue. Closure
of a ticket means that the record was processed, not necessarily that a payment
was received on the same day.

District comparison
-------------------
Aster recorded more enrolled households than Bhagirathi, while Bhagirathi had a
higher share of verified households among its enrolments. The brief does not
give the district-level counts needed to calculate either share precisely, and
it does not define a single overall ranking. It therefore cannot support a claim
that one district performed best without specifying a metric.

Data quality and missing facts
------------------------------
The source systems used different update times, so totals from separate sections
should not be added together. The brief contains no current minister name, no
latest audit conclusion, no national rank, and no exact figure for the current
financial year after 2024-25. Questions asking for those facts should be treated
as unsupported rather than answered from general knowledge.
